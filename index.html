Chameleon: Benchmarking Detection and Backtracking on Commercial-Grade AI-Generated Videos

Abstract

Commercial video generators produce sharper images, steadier motion, and more coherent scene transitions than the open models that shaped early AI-video benchmarks. These changes make synthetic clips harder to separate from captured footage and leave a new forensic question: can a generated video be traced to the real clip that inspired it?

We introduce Chameleon, a benchmark built around these two tasks. It contains 2,300 videos across news, speech, and recommendation domains, pairing 1,700 generated samples with 600 real source videos. The benchmark evaluates authenticity classification and provenance retrieval under realistic variation.

Results

Temporal evidence improves detection, while source retrieval remains a harder step than finding a plausible shortlist.

The video-level DeMamba baseline reaches 80.69% ACC and 92.63% AUC, ahead of the image-level baselines reported in the paper.

The strongest retrieval backbone reaches 0.733 mean R@1 and 0.900 mean R@3.
