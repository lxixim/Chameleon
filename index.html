01WHY CHAMELEON

Benchmarks should reflect the videos people actually encounter.

Research began with open generators. Consumer-facing systems now raise the bar with sharper images, steadier motion and richer scene changes.

The benchmark turns that shift into two checks: classify authenticity, then identify the reference clip behind a synthetic query.

02THE BENCHMARK

04WHAT THE PAPER SHOWS

Temporal evidence
still matters.

The video-level DeMamba baseline reaches 80.69% ACC and 92.63% AUC, ahead of the image-level baselines reported in the paper.

The strongest retrieval backbone reaches 0.733 mean R@1 and 0.900 mean R@3: finding a shortlist is easier than naming the right reference.
