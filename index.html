01WHY CHAMELEON

Benchmarks should reflect the videos people actually encounter.

Open-source generators made AI video research possible. Commercial models now make the outputs harder to separate from reality: higher resolution, stronger temporal coherence and richer scene transitions.

Chameleon turns that gap into two measurable tasks: detect whether a video is synthetic, then trace it back to the real footage that inspired it.

02THE BENCHMARK

04WHAT THE PAPER SHOWS

Temporal evidence
still matters.

On Chameleon, the video-level DeMamba baseline reaches 80.69% ACC and 92.63% AUC, ahead of the image-level baselines reported in the paper.

For backtracking, the strongest backbone reaches 0.733 mean R@1 and 0.900 mean R@3: finding a shortlist is easier than naming the exact source.
